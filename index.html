FAQ

Questions.

Who gets the money?

The developer of the game the coin was launched for. Every creator fee from every coin for that game collects in one escrow tied to its Steam app ID.

What if the developer never claims?

The fees stay in the escrow until they do. Nobody else can take them, including us and the launcher.

How does a developer prove it's their game?

They post a one-time code somewhere only the real developer controls, like a news post on the game's Steam page or its official X account.

What's $INDIE?

$INDIE is the main coin for the Indie launchpad itself. It's the flagship of everything launched here.

Does the launcher earn anything?

No creator fees. You can keep an optional dev buy like any pump.fun launch.

Is this made by Valve or the game's studio?

No. Indie isn't affiliated with Valve, Steam or any studio. Names are only used to say who the fees are for.
